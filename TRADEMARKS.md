## Fine without asking

- Fork, run, study and change the code, and say that your project is based on saxo.dance.
- Share Saxo's videos as they are, crediting @saxo.dance.
- Write about Saxo, review the channel, and make non-commercial fan art.

## Ask first

- Using Saxo, Sadi, Kob or Compote (their names, faces, 3D models, stickers or looks) in your own videos,
  channels, apps, games or products.
- Selling anything that shows them.
- Naming a fork, channel, account, product or domain "Saxo", "saxo.dance" or anything close to them, or giving
  a character a look that could pass for one of them.

A fork that publishes videos brings its own characters and name. To ask, open an issue on
[ClementGuerin/saxo-dance-public](https://github.com/ClementGuerin/saxo-dance-public/issues).
